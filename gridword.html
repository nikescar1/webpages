HOW IT WORKS

the rules
1
Drag to spell
Connect adjacent letters — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal. Words must be 3+ letters. Lift off a tile and return to it to reuse the same letter twice.
2
Tiles take the score of the longest word through them
Every tile in a valid word gets that word's length as its score. A 5-letter word scores each tile 5 points.
3
Beat your old score on a tile by using it in a longer word
A 7-letter word through a tile already scored 5 upgrades it to 7. You only earn the difference.
4
Total score = sum of every tile's score
Cover the board and keep upgrading tiles with longer words to climb higher.
5
Color shows the score
Pink → orange → yellow → green → teal → blue → purple → magenta as words get longer. Rainbow for 12+.
