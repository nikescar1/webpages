Prediction rules & embedded age data

Primary: higher starter median age.

Ties: eldest coach on the four-person staff → coaching-staff median → head coach age → second-oldest coach → third-oldest coach → fourth-oldest coach → alphabetical team name (last-resort deterministic fallback).

The embedded snapshot uses starter median = offense + defense + K + P + KR + PR, counting a player once if they fill multiple roles. Coaching staff = HC, OC, DC and STC. Ages are the supplied Sept. 9, 2026 snapshot.

Schedule source at runtime: ESPN's public NFL scoreboard JSON endpoint. No framework or build step is required.
