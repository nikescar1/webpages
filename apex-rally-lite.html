Credits

Apex Rally Lite — an original, self-contained arcade stage-rally game styled after classic Colin McRae Rally (PS1/PS2 era). All graphics, audio and geometry are generated procedurally in code; there are zero external asset files or network requests.

Renderer

Minimal custom WebGL renderer written from scratch (flat-shaded low-poly + fog).

Physics

Arcade vehicle model with raycast suspension over a procedural heightfield. The physics approach is inspired by crashcat (github.com/isaac-mason/crashcat, MIT) — adapting its rigid-body + collision-manifold concepts — and is not a literal port of crashcat's GJK/EPA engine.

Everything else

Track generation, pace notes, championship AI, Web-Audio SFX, and versioned localStorage saves are all original.
